# Randy's Divine Intervention : The Search for Car Keys

Randy's Divine Intervention : The Search for Car Keys (or shortened to RDITSCK if you like) is a Turn-Based Combat video game where a Humble but Down-On-His-Luck Wizard has a Divination Shop selling Magical Security Systems to Patrons of their Store. 

After another day of minimal customers, Randy goes to the local Tavern to numb the pain through Intoxication. However, upon leaving the Tavern and returning to their vehicle, their Car Keys fall through a Sewer Grate. With no other way to return home, Randy sends his Imbued Weapons to retrieve the Keys and aids them through their Journey through his Magical Surveillance Systems.
